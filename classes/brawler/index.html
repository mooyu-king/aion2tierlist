Important limits

Who should consider 拳星?

A repeatable build-and-spend resource loop for active melee play.

How to approach the class

The empowered state drains rage over time and ends when rage reaches zero. Some attacks change their activation conditions in that state, while listed rage-generating attacks stop restoring rage during it.

A documented example

連攻 - 重擊 - 重捶: An attack chain that restores rage outside the empowered state. 暴走: Enters a rage-consuming state that changes skill behavior. 連環拳: Normally requires a critical hit; that requirement is removed in the empowered state.
