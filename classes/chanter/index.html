FROM CLASS IDENTITY TO PRACTICE

Learn the kit, then test the situation

Close-range attacks, control interactions and a recovery chant connect the combat and support sides of the kit. A secondary healing role should be agreed with the party rather than assumed to replace every dedicated recovery requirement.

Approach and resource recovery

突進擊破

Conditional stun

An attack with a conditional stun effect.

Condition to checkWatch the target’s actual condition after the hit. Player-target incapacitation is chance-based in the reference.

Group recovery chant

痊癒咒語

Restores nearby party health, removes one weakening effect and adds ongoing recovery.

Condition to checkCheck nearby party members: this skill provides immediate recovery, removes one harmful magical effect and continues healing.

READ THE REAL INTERFACE

Find the condition before changing the skill

A skill’s activation requirement and a mobile-assistance setting are different checks. A health threshold does not bypass a required buff, successful block or critical hit.

  1. A · Skill description & specializations

    Read the left-hand tooltip. Compare the selected specialization with the conditions on the skill cards above; a different specialization may change the behavior.

  2. B · Assistance priority & delay

    The upper-right controls set selection priority and a delay after cooldown. They do not demonstrate that a class-specific trigger is currently active.

  3. C · Health & resource thresholds

    The lower-right controls show health and resource percentages, with “above” and “below” choices. The example has “below” selected. Choose deliberately rather than copying the pictured values.

MAKE THE TRADE-OFF VISIBLE

Compare Chanter with Cleric

Support through combat or concentrate on recovery?

Chanter suits a player who wants attacks and party support to share attention. Compare Cleric if you prefer recovery and cleansing to drive your decisions. A support label alone does not establish that either kit meets every healing requirement.
