FROM CLASS IDENTITY TO PRACTICE

Learn the kit, then test the situation

Different spirits provide different interactions. The earth spirit builds threat, the water spirit can restore the caster’s spirit resource, and the wind spirit offers recovery. Summoning also connects to an elemental accumulation mechanic.

Earth spirit and threat

召喚：地之精靈

Summons an earth spirit whose attacks build threat.

Condition to checkObserve which target the spirit attacks and the enemy’s response. Pressing the summon again dismisses it.

Water spirit and resources

召喚：水之精靈

Summons a water spirit with resource-restoring attack interactions.

Condition to checkYour resource recovery follows the water spirit’s successful attacks; summoning alone is not the recovery event.

Four-element consumption

元素融合

Requires the four-element state obtained through summoning; using it consumes that state.

Condition to checkRequires the four-element effect. Casting consumes it; additional elements cannot accumulate while that state is active.

READ THE REAL INTERFACE

Find the condition before changing the skill

A skill’s activation requirement and a mobile-assistance setting are different checks. A health threshold does not bypass a required buff, successful block or critical hit.

  1. A · Skill description & specializations

    Read the left-hand tooltip. Compare the selected specialization with the conditions on the skill cards above; a different specialization may change the behavior.

  2. B · Assistance priority & delay

    The upper-right controls set selection priority and a delay after cooldown. They do not demonstrate that a class-specific trigger is currently active.

  3. C · Health & resource thresholds

    The lower-right controls show health and resource percentages, with “above” and “below” choices. The example has “below” selected. Choose deliberately rather than copying the pictured values.

MAKE THE TRADE-OFF VISIBLE

Compare Spiritmaster with Sorcerer

Manage spirits or focus on your own spell sequence?

Spiritmaster adds different spirit functions and elemental accumulation to your decisions. Sorcerer centers the comparison on spell conditions and elemental attacks, without that same spirit-management focus.

Before you choose: Read the earth and water spirit functions. If choosing between those tools interests you, explore Spiritmaster before comparing spell sequences.
