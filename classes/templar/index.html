YOUR FIRST CHARACTER

Is Templar right for you?

Choose it if…

You want to protect the party with a shield and control enemy positioning.

Expect to learn

Watch your allies as well as your own health; learn when to pull and block.

Avoid this first mistake

Moving or turning an enemy into teammates while trying to protect them.

FROM CLASS IDENTITY TO PRACTICE

Learn the kit, then test the situation

Threat attacks, a pull and block-triggered effects support frontline control. Some control effects explicitly have different conditions for NPCs and players, so a reliable PvE action may behave differently in PvP.

READ THE REAL INTERFACE

Find the condition before changing the skill

A skill’s activation requirement and a mobile-assistance setting are different checks. A health threshold does not bypass a required buff, successful block or critical hit.

  1. A · Skill description & specializations

    Read the left-hand tooltip. Compare the selected specialization with the conditions on the skill cards above; a different specialization may change the behavior.

  2. B · Assistance priority & delay

    The upper-right controls set selection priority and a delay after cooldown. They do not demonstrate that a class-specific trigger is currently active.

  3. C · Health & resource thresholds

    The lower-right controls show health and resource percentages, with “above” and “below” choices. The example has “below” selected. Choose deliberately rather than copying the pictured values.

MAKE THE TRADE-OFF VISIBLE

Compare Templar with Gladiator

Control the frontline or focus on melee attacks?

Templar suits a player interested in threat, pulls and successful-block follow-ups. Gladiator is worth comparing if attacks and knockdown opportunities are your priority, with defensive tools supporting that approach.

Before you choose: Watch which responsibility you naturally notice first: an enemy moving away from the group or an opening to attack.
