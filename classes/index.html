Gladiator

Weapon

Close range
Primary role
Melee fighter

Gladiator combines greatsword attacks with a durable frontline identity. NC describes it primarily as a melee attacker that can also take an off-tank role. Choose it for active close-range combat, then decide with your party who is responsible for enemy attention.

Close range

Shield tank

Templar is the shield-focused tank in NC’s class guide. Its job involves more than surviving: keeping a useful position lets allies attack without constantly chasing or avoiding the same enemy.

Assassin uses daggers, stealth and positional attacks. Its official description emphasizes approaching from concealment and attacking from behind. It suits a player who enjoys creating an opening rather than standing in one place.
