AION 2 Ranger

Ranger uses a bow to fight at range. The official guide links this distance to battlefield awareness: the useful question is whether you can maintain a firing position while responding to what is moving toward you.

Strengths & role considerations

Ranged attacks allow you to choose a firing position away from the immediate front line.

Important limits

Distance can also put you outside convenient healing or support range.

Who should consider Ranger?

Mobile shots and slowing effects offer tools for controlling the distance of a pull.

How to approach the class

Some arrows can be used while moving, while other attacks are charged. A movement attack grants a precision state that interacts with follow-up skills; slows and traps add positioning options.

A documented example

套索箭: An attack that applies a movement-slowing effect. 目標箭: Moves the character in the chosen direction and grants a precision state. 瞄準箭: A charged attack with an additional benefit during the precision state.
