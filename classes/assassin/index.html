AION 2 Assassin

Assassin uses daggers, stealth and positional attacks. Its official description emphasizes approaching from concealment and attacking from behind. It suits a player who enjoys creating an opening rather than standing in one place.

What to look for

Follow the approach to each target and the repositioning between attacks. Consider whether you enjoy committing to melee range and reacting quickly when an opening closes.

Strengths & role considerations

Back-attack interactions reward deliberate positioning behind a stable target.

Important limits

Chasing positional damage during a mechanic can cost more than the bonus is worth.

Who should consider Assassin?

A mobile approach with conditional attacks for players who enjoy active execution.

How to approach the class

Marks can be built and consumed for an attack with additional control potential. Other attacks activate after a critical hit or an evade. Learn these activation conditions before treating every skill as always available.

A documented example

暗襲: Moves behind a target and has a conditional stun interaction. 突襲: Gains damage when delivered from behind. 紋樣爆炸: Uses accumulated marks to improve its damage and stun chance.
