Entry time, activity duration and different rift activities

The Taiwan guide describes a 15-minute entry window for the general rift and a separate 10-minute passage for the contention activity. Neither is a personal activity-duration timer. Confirm the activity and current regional rules in game before crossing.

The guide also describes enemy-territory sealed dungeons and camp tasks, with player combat through PvP mode. Choose your objective and team before entering.
