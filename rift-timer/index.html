TAIWAN OFFICIAL GUIDE · READ BEFORE ENTERING

From the opening message to enemy territory

01 · LOCATE

Check the in-game map

The game displays a system message when a passage opens. Open the map to find its generated location; a timer cannot identify the live portal for you.

02 · ENTER

Check the right activity

The general rift section describes a 15-minute passage and an entry capacity. A full passage can prevent entry even while the opening window remains.

03 · PLAN

Choose your objective

The guide describes enemy-territory sealed dungeons and camp tasks, with player combat available through PvP mode. Confirm your objective and team before crossing.

Why do some references say 10 minutes?

The same Taiwan guide has a separate contention activity section with a 10-minute passage. Its activity schedule and participation conditions must not be substituted for the general rift. A Korean or international guide can also refer to a different version.
