AION 2 Patch Notes

November 11, 2026 · Global Patch Highlights

Reviewed November 13

Patch at a Glance

Dungeons
Ludra runs now allow two hours on Normal and Hard. Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den open on Hard at item level 2,800.
Consumables
Potions, food and scrolls that previously lasted five minutes now last ten. Those effects persist through death and revival.
Weekly rewards
Each of the three Way of the Transcended weekly mission steps awards one bound Unique Sync Stone. Complete all three for three stones per week.
Abyss earnings
Kina dropped by Lower Abyss monsters is now bound. In Chaotic Outer Reshanta, you can earn more Abyss Points.
↗ Official notice

11 NOV 2026 · PATCH

More time inside Abyssal Forge

On both Normal and Hard, your party has twice as long to finish Ludra. The central reactor also has less health. Use the extra time for a learning run; two hours is the limit, not the expected clear time.

The guarantee applies to the reward group. It does not promise every item in that group on each run.

↗ Official notice
11 NOV 2026 · EXPEDITIONS

Can you enter the new Hard modes?

Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn Den now offer Hard difficulty. Both require item level 2,800. The boss Frenzy timer is four minutes and each player gets up to three revives. Agree on recovery duties before entering.

Enter your level to compare it with the entry requirement.
↗ Official notice

Does this change my class or rift plan?

The two-hour figure belongs to a dungeon run. Do not use it to predict when a rift opens. These dungeon adjustments alone are also insufficient evidence for a class tier change.

Earlier notices

4 Nov 2026 · Hotfix — early Hard-mode listing

The two expeditions showed a Hard option before it was intended. The fix removed that misleading listing; players were instructed to update and restart the client. The 11 November notice above is the actual release.
