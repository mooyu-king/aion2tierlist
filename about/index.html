About AION2 Tier List

Learn what aion2tierlist.org does, who maintains its content and how its class and rift tools handle uncertainty.

Two tasks, one focused website

AION2 Tier List helps players investigate a class choice and plan a Spacetime Rift occurrence. The class directory, comparison and rift planner can be used independently. We do not require an account, a quiz or a purchase to reach the basic information.

Who maintains the site

The site owner maintains the content and source records. Editorial work is public-source analysis unless a particular record explicitly says otherwise. We do not claim a player-testing team or professional credentials. The responsible contact is mooyuking@gmail.com.

Independent from the game publisher

This is a community website at aion2tierlist.org. It is not operated, endorsed or sponsored by NCSOFT. AION 2 names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Our wing-and-rift website mark is an original site design, not an official game logo.

What we promise to label

A known class name, a performance judgment and a calculated opening are different things. We identify the service and the evidence limits, preserve source dates and keep unknown values unknown. We do not provide live portal positions, occupancy or account eligibility.

Help improve the record

Send a correction with a source, service, version and observation time. Please do not send your game password, payment information or private account credentials. Feedback is reviewed before public content changes.
