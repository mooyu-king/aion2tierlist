How should I use these opinions to choose a class?

Game8 examines damage, survival, utility and ease of use separately. PlayerAuctions weighs several activities together. A lower overall letter therefore cannot tell you whether a party still needs the role.

For your own shortlist, identify your party responsibility, then compare positioning and resource requirements in our official-guide-based class profiles. Treat difficulty as something to test with the practice checklist, rather than a promise that one class will suit everyone.

These articles describe Cleric differently. We retain the official healing identity instead of adopting conflicting combat-role labels. Neither source covers 拳星 in these lists.

Why can two sites rank the same class differently?

A list can measure editorial preference, party contribution or population. Before following a letter grade, check the service, activity, patch and assessment method.

Check the activity

Aion2TierList.com separates PvE, PvP and Solo. Its methodology distinguishes editorial assessment from statistics such as population and combat power.

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers

Which AION 2 class is S tier?

In the Game8 snapshot checked on 10 October 2026, Gladiator and Ranger appear in S tier for both PvE and PvP. Other placements depend on the selected mode. These are attributed source opinions, not our own regional patch tests; Solo and Brawler are not ranked by this source. Read the role trade-offs before choosing.

Are Taiwan, Korea and international ratings interchangeable?

No. Updates and available classes can differ. Select the service you actually play; unknown regional facts are not filled with another service’s data.

What does following a class do?

It saves a preference on this browser and lets you filter the list. It does not create an account or send background notifications.

How is the tier list researched?

Class facts, comparative judgments and time rules have separate evidence records. Read the methodology and source limitations before relying on an assessment.
